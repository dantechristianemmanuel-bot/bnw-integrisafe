What we inspect, test, and certify

One provider for your equipment compliance — from heavy equipment, lifting equipment and accessories, boilers and pressure vessels, to warehouse racking systems and manufacturing machinery.

✓ All certifications are signed and sealed by a licensed Professional Mechanical Engineer.

Heavy Equipment Inspection

Cranes, forklifts, excavators, payloaders, graders, and other construction heavy equipment.

You get: signed test report, certificate, and a PASSED sticker attached to the unit

Load Testing & Lifting Gear

Overhead cranes, hoists, chain blocks, and lifting accessories such as slings, shackles, and hooks.

You get: signed load test report, certificate, and inspection sticker or tag

Non-Destructive Testing

Ultrasonic thickness gauging (UTG), penetrant testing (PT), and magnetic particle inspection (MPI) by Level 2 practitioners.

You get: signed NDT report, certificate of NDT, and engineering evaluation for acceptance, remaining life, and factor of safety (as applicable)

Hydrostatic Testing

Pressure testing of vessels, piping, hoses, and fire lines to verify integrity and leak-tightness.

You get: signed hydrostatic test report and certificate

DOLE PTO / CEI Support

Inspection, testing, and as-built plans for boilers, pressure vessels, air receivers, gensets, power piping, elevators, hoists, electrical systems, and other mechanical equipment.

You get: test results, as-built plans and drawings, and documents for your DOLE application

Storage Racking Inspection

Checks on warehouse pallet racking for damage, overloading, and safe installation.

You get: signed inspection report with recommendations and a storage racking certificate

Safety Training

Customized courses for workers and supervisors, onsite or online — from equipment inspection to OSH topics tailored to your site.

You get: certificate of completion, training manuals and materials, plus free consultancy during the training

Engineering Consultancy

Guidance on equipment safety, compliance gaps, and documentation from a licensed PME.

You get: written findings and recommendations, signed and sealed by a PME

FAQ

Common questions

Does our construction heavy equipment need DOLE testing and certification?

Yes. Under DOLE Department Order No. 13, Section 10, heavy equipment used in construction must be tested and certified by a DOLE-accredited testing organization. BNW IntegriSafe is accredited by DOLE-OSHC for this.

Do boilers and air receivers need a Permit to Operate?

Generally, yes. Under the Occupational Safety and Health Standards (Rule 1160 for boilers and Rule 1170 for unfired pressure vessels such as air receivers), these require inspection and a Permit to Operate from DOLE. We perform the testing and prepare the supporting documents for your application.

What do you need from us to prepare a quote?

The equipment type, capacity, quantity, site location, and your target schedule. Photos of the nameplates help us quote faster and more accurately.

Do you serve sites outside Metro Manila?

Yes. We mobilize nationwide. Travel and mobilization are included in your quotation based on the site location.

What will we receive after the inspection?

A signed inspection or test report for each item, and a certificate where applicable. For PTO and CEI work, we also provide the documents needed for your DOLE application.
